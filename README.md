# Blanca

Blanca is a clipboard manager for [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org) Linux. It
keeps a history of the text you have cut or copied, so you can get back to a
snippet, a URL, or that sentence you had _just right_ five minutes ago, even
after you have copied something else.

Blanca is a port of [Jumpcut](https://github.com/snark/jumpcut), the
long-running macOS clipboard manager by Steve Cook, rebuilt for Wayland and
Hyprland. It is named after my dog.

## Status

Early development. Nothing is usable yet.

## Goals

- Lightweight and 100% focused on the clipboard, in the spirit of Jumpcut.
- Native to Omarchy: Wayland clipboard access, Hyprland keybinds, and a
  bezel that fits the Omarchy look.
- Plain text first. Clippings are stored locally and never leave your
  machine.
